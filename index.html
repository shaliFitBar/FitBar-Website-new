Allied Health Collaboration

Your GP cleared you
to train. Now what?

Managing a health condition, recovering from injury, or living with a chronic illness does not mean exercise is off the table. In most cases, it is exactly what is needed — when it is prescribed correctly for your body.

How It Works
  • 01 Shalika obtains clearance and any guidance from your GP, physio, or specialist before designing your program.
  • 02 Every session, every program, every nutrition recommendation is built in accordance with your medical team's advice.
  • 03 Ongoing communication with your allied health team as your condition and capacity evolve.
  • 04 Your health fund may provide rebates — Shalika is AUSactive Accredited. Check your extras cover.
Who This Is For

Managing type 2 diabetes, hypertension, or cardiovascular conditions

Recovering from surgery, injury, or musculoskeletal conditions

Living with chronic pain, arthritis, or osteoporosis

Post-cancer treatment returning to physical activity

FitBar coaching is not medical treatment. All programs require medical clearance and operate in coordination with your healthcare team — never as a substitute for it.
